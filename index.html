Visas

  • Subclass 417 — Working Holiday visa
  • Subclass 462 — Work and Holiday visa

To apply for a second or third visa, holders must usually complete a prescribed minimum period of specified work in certain industries and areas. Home Affairs describes this as at least 3 months (88 calendar days) of specified work on a first visa for a second visa (rising to 120 days from 1 March 2027 unless the work is completed and a valid application lodged by 28 February 2027), and at least 6 months (179 calendar days) of specified work on a second visa for a third visa. The two visas do not always use the same area rules. UK passport holders who lodge a 417 application on or after 1 July 2024 do not need specified work for a second or third 417 visa.

Policy update (announced 17 November 2026)

  • Complete your specified work and lodge a valid application by 28 February 2027: you can apply under the current process (88 days for a second visa).
  • From 1 March 2027: a second visa needs 120 days (4 months) of specified work; work done before then still counts. A third visa still needs 6 months.
  • From 1 March 2027: applicants for a second or third visa (except UK passport holders) must register and be invited (Next Year Regional Work Guarantee). Registration opens 1 December 2026.
  • The kinds of specified work and the postcode lists have not changed (re-checked 22 November 2026).

FAQ

What is specified work?
Specified work is work undertaken in a specified industry and area of Australia. The kinds of work and the areas are set by Home Affairs and by legal instruments LIN 22/012 (417) and LIN 22/013 (462). Always confirm the current rules on the official Home Affairs pages.
What changes on 1 March 2027?
A second visa will need 120 days (4 months) of specified work instead of 88 days; work done before 1 March 2027 still counts. A third visa still needs 6 months. If you complete your specified work and lodge a valid application by 28 February 2027, you can apply under the current process.
Do I need to register before applying for a second or third visa?
From 1 March 2027, yes, except UK passport holders. Register your interest (registration opens 1 December 2026); if invited, you receive a Next Year Regional Work Guarantee and have 12 months to apply. Registering does not guarantee an invitation or a visa.
集簽郵遞區號要看住的地方，還是工作地點？
要看實際工作的地點。請用農場、工地或工作單位的郵遞區號查詢。
Why are 417 and 462 results different?
The two visas do not have the same specified-work rules. For subclass 462, mining is not specified work anywhere in Australia, and fishing and pearling and tree farming and felling count only in Northern Australia.
Does my home address postcode matter?
No. What matters is where the work is actually done. Use the postcode of the farm, site or business where you work.
I have a UK passport. Do I need specified work?
For the subclass 417 visa, UK passport holders who lodge an application on or after 1 July 2024 do not need to complete specified work for a second or third Working Holiday visa. See the Home Affairs arrangements for UK passport holders.
